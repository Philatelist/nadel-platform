A plot owner sees and downloads only the documents of their own plots. A document of someone else's plot is not found by the system.

## The invoice for a month

The board makes the invoice. You can get it by e-mail, in Telegram, on paper, or download it yourself.

1. Open your [account](help:account), the {{ui:invoices.myTitle}} block.
2. At the top is the latest invoice of each of your plots. Older months are hidden under "{{ui:invoices.myOlder}}".
3. Press {{ui:invoices.download}} and the PDF is saved on your device.

If the board issued the invoice again, you will see a new version with the same number. How to read an invoice: [How to read my invoice](help:member-bill).

## The cash receipt

When you hand cash over at the cooperative's till, the treasurer writes a receipt. It has a running number without gaps. The receipt is internal, it is not a fiscal check. Next to such a payment in the account of the plot there is a "Receipt" link.

## The reconciliation act

The reconciliation act is a PDF for your plot and the chosen period. It holds:

- the cooperative, the plot and its owners;
- the balance at the start of the period: debt, overpayment or nothing;
- all charges, corrections, reversals and payments by dates;
- the balance at the end and a sentence about the state of payments on the last date;
- two places for signatures: from the cooperative and from the member.

How to download the act:

1. Open your [account](help:account), the {{ui:ownership.myPlots}} block.
2. Next to the right plot press {{ui:reconciliationAct.open}}.
3. In the {{ui:reconciliationAct.from}} and {{ui:reconciliationAct.to}} fields set the period. The start cannot be earlier than the first charge of the plot, and the end cannot be later than today.
4. Press {{ui:reconciliationAct.download}}.

The act is not stored on the server: it is built on request, and the same period always gives the same act. If the requisites of the cooperative are not entered yet, the act cannot be built: ask the board.

## The certificate of payment status

The certificate of debt or no debt is issued by the board. An owner does not download it alone: ask the board to issue one.
