## Signing in

Open the address of the system that the board gave you and press {{ui:auth.signIn}}. Signing in happens on a separate sign-in page. After signing in you land on the home page; what it shows depends on your rights.

If signing in fails, the system says: "{{ui:auth.signInFailed}}". Try again. If it still does not work, contact the chairman of the cooperative.

## The header and the menu

In the header, on the left, is the name of your cooperative, and on the right your name. The name leads to "{{ui:nav.account}}". Next to it are the language switch, the theme switch and the {{ui:auth.signOut}} button.

The menu on the left shows only the sections that are open to you. The chairman sees everything, the treasurer less, a cooperative member the least. What is missing from the menu is not meant for you: the system does not show a section you have no access to.

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the {{ui:nav.menu}} button.

## Language and theme

The language is switched with the switch in the header: {{ui:language.name}} or Ukrainian. The system remembers the choice in this browser. The theme has three options: {{ui:theme.system}}, {{ui:theme.light}} and {{ui:theme.dark}}.

## The help

The help opens with the "{{ui:help.title}}" item in the menu. It has contents by section, and each page explains one part of the system. You see only the pages about the screens you can use.

On a help page a list of headings is shown on the right: it helps you jump to the place you need.
