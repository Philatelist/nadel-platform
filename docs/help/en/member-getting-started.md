This page is for a cooperative member, that is, a plot owner. It has everything you need to know first. If you work on the board, see [Getting started](help:getting-started) for the board.

## How to sign in

Open the address of the system given by the board and press {{ui:auth.signIn}}. On the sign-in page enter your e-mail and password. There is no self-registration: the board invites you, and a letter "{{ui:setPassword.title}}" with a link arrives at your e-mail. The link is valid for 72 hours and works once; you set the password on the [set-password page](help:set-password) and then sign in with your e-mail. If the link has expired, ask the board to send a new one. If signing in fails or the system does not find you among the members, contact the board: perhaps a different e-mail is in your member card.

## Your home page

After signing in you land on "{{ui:nav.account}}". It is your home screen and the first menu item; the logo in the header always returns here. Everything a member needs is on this screen: your plots, charges and payments, meters, invoices and notifications. The list of all the cooperative's plots is not shown to members. You open the card of your plot from the account by pressing its number, and the "{{ui:plot.backToAccount}}" link returns. In detail: [My account](help:account).

## What you can do

- Submit a meter reading: in the {{ui:readings.mine.title}} block press {{ui:readings.mine.submit}}. The board checks the reading. Step by step: [How to submit a meter reading](help:member-readings).
- Get an invoice: in the {{ui:invoices.myTitle}} block press {{ui:invoices.download}}. How to read an invoice: [How to read my invoice](help:member-bill). The reconciliation act and other documents: [Documents for a member](help:member-documents).
- Connect Telegram so that invoices arrive in the messenger: the {{ui:telegramLink.connect}} button in the account. Step by step: [How to connect Telegram](help:member-telegram).
- Install Nadel on your phone and get notifications on the device: [Install on your phone](help:member-install) and [Notifications on your phone](help:member-push).

You see only your own data. The system does not show other people's plots, amounts or owners.

## The help

The "?" button in the header opens the help for the screen you are on. It appears at the side, next to the screen, which stays usable. If you move to another screen, the panel shows the help for that screen. The "{{ui:help.close}}" button or the Esc key closes the panel, and the "{{ui:help.openFull}}" button opens the page on its own. On a phone the help takes the whole screen, and the "{{ui:help.back}}" button returns to the screen. A member has no "{{ui:help.title}}" item in the menu: the help opens with the "?" button.
